# One More Bounce - Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 11, 2026*

This policy describes how the mobile game **One More Bounce** ("the Game"), published by Damian-Ion Gheorghisenco ("we"), handles information. The Game requires no account or registration.

## Information stored on your device

Your level progress, medals, settings and the age range you select on first launch are stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email address, birthday or precise location.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional rewarded ads when you ask for a hint. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings, Privacy options. Players who indicate they are under the adult age range receive only non-personalized ads with child-directed settings. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

For players in the adult age range only, the Game may send anonymous usage statistics (for example, levels started and completed) and crash reports through Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, to help us improve the Game. This data does not identify you personally. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## In-app purchases

If the Game offers purchases, they are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details.

## Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. For players who select a younger age range, analytics are disabled and ads are limited to non-personalized, child-appropriate content.

## Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no account data, uninstalling the Game removes all data stored by the Game on your device. To request deletion of data held by our advertising or analytics providers, contact us using the address below.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
