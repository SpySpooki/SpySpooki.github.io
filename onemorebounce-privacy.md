# One More Bounce - Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 12, 2026*

This policy describes how the mobile game **One More Bounce** ("the Game"), published by Damian-Ion Gheorghisenco ("we"), handles information. The Game requires no account or registration.

## Information stored on your device

Your level progress, medals, settings, the items you have bought and the age range you select on first launch are stored only on your device. We do not collect your name, email address, birthday or precise location.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional rewarded ads when you ask for a hint. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings, Privacy options. Players who indicate they are under the adult age range receive only non-personalized ads with child-directed settings. If you own the Hint Pass, the Game shows no ads and requests none. More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

These services run only for players in the adult age range (18 or older). For younger age ranges they are turned off and nothing is sent.

**Anonymous analytics** (Firebase Analytics) are off unless you turn them on in Settings, Share anonymous analytics. When on, the Game sends usage statistics such as levels started and completed, shots fired and hints used.

**Crash reports** (Firebase Crashlytics) are on by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings, Share crash reports. When the Game crashes or hits a serious error, a report is sent with the error details and stack trace, the device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier created by Firebase. It contains no name, contact details or advertising ID.

We use this data only to fix problems and improve the Game. It is not used for advertising and does not identify you personally. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## In-app purchases

The Game offers two optional one-time purchases: the **Hint Pass** (unlimited hints with no ads) and the **Supporter Pack** (every ball skin and trail). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms; we never receive your payment details. The Game stores on your device which items you own, and asks the store again when you use Settings, Shop, Restore purchases (on Android this also happens automatically at launch).

## Children

The Game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. For players who select a younger age range, analytics and crash reports are disabled and ads are limited to non-personalized, child-appropriate content.

## Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no account data, uninstalling the Game removes all data stored by the Game on your device. To request deletion of data held by our advertising or analytics providers, contact us using the address below.
