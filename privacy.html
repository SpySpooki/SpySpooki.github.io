Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This policy applies to mobile apps and games published by DamianGh. The details below describe PopBirdy; features and third-party services in other DamianGh apps may differ.

PopBirdy and age range

PopBirdy is intended for players of different ages, including children. On first launch, it asks you to select an age range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and older). It does not ask for your birth date. The selected range is stored on your device and is used to apply age-appropriate advertising settings. The game does not send your selected range to a DamianGh server.

Advertising and data

PopBirdy uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads through AdMob mediation for optional rewarded ads and other in-game advertising. PopBirdy is configured to request non-personalized advertising across age ranges and to apply additional child and teen treatment where appropriate. Selecting an age range does not change gameplay progress.

Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. When advertising services operate, Google and Unity may process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, approximate location derived from network information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. They may use cookies or similar device storage where permitted. Such data can be used for delivering and measuring ads, limiting repetition, protecting against fraud, and operating their services. DamianGh does not directly receive your name, birth date, or contact details from normal gameplay.

For the providers' data practices and controls, see Google Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Privacy choices

Where required, PopBirdy uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices. If a privacy-choice control is available for your region and age range, you can revisit your choices in the game's Settings. Declining advertising consent does not prevent you from playing. Ads are requested only when the applicable advertising configuration and consent state permit them. Choosing to consent does not enable personalized advertising in PopBirdy.

Children's privacy

PopBirdy applies child-directed treatment for younger players and age-specific restrictions for teens. The game's first-run age-range choice is kept on the device. Advertising providers may still process limited data as described above, subject to the applicable age and privacy settings. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy question, contact us using the address below.
